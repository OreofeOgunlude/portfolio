HAZOP Assistant

A focused workflow tool for conducting and reviewing HAZOP studies. Built around the actual methodology used in chemical and petrochemical plants: define nodes, walk through deviation guide words, document causes and consequences, identify safeguards, generate audit-ready reports.

Process Safety OSHA 1910.119 CSA Z767 PHA Workflow

HAZOP studies are required every five years under OSHA Process Safety Management (29 CFR 1910.119) and the equivalent Canadian standard CSA Z767. For small and mid-sized plants, conducting them is expensive, time-consuming, and often delayed past compliance windows. HAZOP Assistant targets a 40 to 60 percent reduction in study duration with improved consistency and audit defensibility.

Fourth-year process design project at Western University. Designed a hybrid hydrometallurgical-electrochemical process for recovering rare-earth elements from end-of-life electronic waste, awarded first place by a panel of industry judges.

🏆 First place, 18 graduating teams

Rare-Earth Element Recovery from Electronic Waste

Western University · Final year capstone · 2024 to 2025
CEAB-accredited chemical engineering program
Process flow diagram (panoramic)

The problem

Rare-earth elements are critical for permanent magnets, batteries, and electronics, but global supply is concentrated in a small number of countries. Electronic waste contains significant REE quantities that are currently landfilled rather than recovered.

The approach

The team designed a hybrid hydrometallurgical-electrochemical process integrating mechanical pre-treatment, acid leaching, selective separation, and electrochemical recovery. The hybrid design improved separation selectivity and reduced reagent consumption compared to conventional pure-hydrometallurgical approaches.

What was delivered

A complete process design package: mass and energy balances, equipment specifications and sizing, an economic analysis with capital and operating cost estimates, environmental and safety considerations, and recommendations for further development.

Full technical report available on request. Detailed mass balances, equipment specifications, and economic analysis can be shared with potential employers under appropriate confidentiality agreements.
